
There are a selection of these for the scenario:

| RULE TWEAK             | HOW TO WORK IT                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | WHY YOU NEED IT                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Clarence Will Not Wait | Until the English archers have arrived, the English leader is Impetuous. If the Leader's unit has a legal Attack action against an enemy in line of sight and within reach, it must attempt to Attack before any non-mounted English unit is activated. The English player may still activate another mounted unit first.                        | This rule represents Clarence ignoring counsel and hurrying into action. The historical sources explicitly describe him attacking quickly and brushing aside warnings about the absence of his archers.                           |
| Delayed Archers        | The English Archers and one off-table Heavy Infantry unit arrive as reinforcements. From Turn 3 onwards, roll 1D6 at the start of the English turn: 4+: the Heavy Infantry arrives from the western table edge; 5+: the Archers arrive from the western table edge. If the English Leader is slain, both arrivals are delayed by one extra turn. | This rule is the heart of the scenario: the English hit hard early, but their best supporting arm is late. That matches the historical account that many English archers were absent or not yet assembled when Clarence attacked. |
| Bridge Guards          | The allied bridge archers count as being in hard cover while defending the bridge approaches until they move or are charged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Historically, Scots archers were posted to guard the bridge and fired on the English as they forced the crossing.                                                                                                                 |
| Refuge in the Church   | The first time an English unit crosses the bridge, one Franco-Scottish infantry unit within 6' of the church may make a free move directly into the church enclosure (if not already there).                                                                                                                                                     | This nods to the brief historical episode in which defenders fell back around the church before the fight spilled onward.                                                                                                         |
| Main Allied Line       | The first time an English unit moves more than 6' east of the church, the Franco-Scottish player may immediately reveal any hidden units and place them in legal positions east of the church/ village line.                                                                                                                                     | This represents Clarence breaking through the initial resistance only to discover the real allied strength ahead.                                                                                                                 |
| Death of Clarence      | If the English Leader is killed, captured, or routed off-table: all English units immediately take a Courage test and for the rest of the game, English units suffer -1 to future Courage tests.                                                                                                                                                 | The real battle collapsed after Clarence was killed, with the English losing heart and cohesion.                                                                                                                                  |
