## SPECIAL RULES 

There are a selection of these for the scenario: 

|**RULE TWEAK**|**HOW TO WORK IT**|**WHY YOU NEED IT**|
|---|---|---|
|Clarence Will<br>Not Wait|Until the English archers have arrived, the English<br>leader is Impetuous. If the Leader’s unit has a<br>legal Attack action against an enemy in line of<br>sight and within reach, it must attempt to Attack<br>before any non-mounted English unit is activated.<br>The English player may still activate another<br>mounted unit first.|<br>This rule represents<br>Clarence ignoring counsel<br>and hurrying into action. The<br>historical sources explicitly<br>describe him attacking<br>quickly and brushing aside<br>warnings about the absence<br>of his archers.|
|Delayed<br>Archers|The English Archers and one off-table Heavy<br>Infantry unit arrive as reinforcements. From Turn<br>3 onwards, roll 1D6 at the start of the English<br>turn: 4+: the Heavy Infantry arrives from the<br>western table edge; 5+: the Archers arrive from<br>the western table edge. If the English Leader is<br>slain, both arrivals are delayed by one extra turn.|This rule is the heart of the<br>scenario: the English hit<br>hard early, but their best<br>supporting arm is late.<br>That matches the historical<br>account that many English<br>archers were absent or<br>not yet assembled when<br>Clarence attacked.|
|Bridge Guards|The allied bridge archers count as being in hard<br>cover while defending the bridge approaches<br>until they move or are charged.|Historically, Scots archers<br>were posted to guard the<br>bridge and fired on the<br>English as they forced the<br>crossing.|
|Refuge in the<br>Church|The first time an English unit crosses the bridge,<br>one Franco-Scottish infantry unit within 6” of the<br>church may make a free move directly into the<br>church enclosure (if not already there).|This nods to the brief<br>historical episode in which<br>defenders fell back around<br>the church before the fight<br>spilled onward.|
|Main Allied Line|The first time an English unit moves more than 6”<br>east of the church, the Franco-Scottish player<br>may immediately reveal any hidden units and<br>place them in legal positions east of the church/<br>village line.|This represents Clarence<br>breaking through the initial<br>resistance only to discover<br>the real allied strength<br>ahead.|
|Death of<br>Clarence|If the English Leader is killed, captured, or routed<br>off-table: all English units immediately take<br>a Courage test and for the rest of the game,<br>English units suffer -1 to future Courage tests.|The real battle collapsed<br>after Clarence was killed,<br>with the English losing heart<br>and cohesion.|
